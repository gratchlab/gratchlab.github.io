About

Jonathan Gratch
Research Professor · Director, Affective Computing Group

Jonathan Gratch is a Research Professor at Vanderbilt University's College of Connected Computing, where he directs the Affective Computing Group. His research examines the interplay of emotion, cognition, and social behavior, developing computational models that power virtual humans capable of emotionally intelligent interaction. His work spans affective computing, cognitive modeling, human-computer interaction, virtual humans, and persuasive technology, with applications in negotiation, therapy, and training. He was previously Director for Virtual Human Research at the USC Institute for Creative Technologies.
